## 1. Email en inglés

**Subject:** Request for proposal: fixed-fee PRC regulatory memo for a milestone-based technical services platform (Hong Kong / mainland China)

Dear [Firm name] team,

We are a start-up preparing to launch **Qiao**, a curated platform through which companies outside mainland China (mainly Asia-Pacific, the Gulf and Europe) procure **milestone-based software and technical services** from engineers and teams based in mainland China. We would like to invite your firm to submit a **fixed-fee proposal** for a written legal memorandum on the questions listed below.

**The model in brief.**
- A Hong Kong company (to be incorporated) sells the services to the client and collects prepayment for each milestone (Stripe).
- In phase 1, a PRC software-outsourcing company (the "Partner") is the actual buyer of each engineer's services. It contracts each engineer on a deliverable basis (no fixed hours, no exclusivity), pays in RMB, withholds individual income tax, handles real-name verification, registers technology-export contracts and invoices the Hong Kong company for an export of services on a cost-plus basis. The Partner never just collects and forwards funds.
- The platform publishes **projects only, never job postings**: every project has milestones, deliverables and an end date (12 months maximum). Clients see pseudonymized engineer profiles; a full CV is shared only with the engineer's consent for that specific project. ID documents and bank details stay with the Partner in mainland China.
- Certain categories are prohibited (cryptocurrency, semiconductors and chip design, drones, mapping and geodata, defence, government or SOE data, expert networks, research assistance and data collection, rare earths, lithium and batteries, biometric and genomic data).
- In phase 2, if the pilot succeeds, a WFOE in a Shenzhen pilot zone would take over the Partner's role.

**Questions.**
1. **HR services licence.** Does a project-only marketplace that uses technical-service contracts and publishes no job postings fall outside the HR service licence regime (Interim Regulations on the Human Resources Market, State Council Decree No. 700; Provisions on the Administration of HR Service Agencies, MOHRSS Decree No. 50; the January 2026 five-ministry notice on recruitment information on online platforms)? Which features would change that conclusion (e.g. visible profiles, team applications, long or repeated engagements)?
2. **Telecom licences.** Which value-added telecom licences would the platform require (B21 and/or B25, and which B25 sub-category)? Can a WFOE in a Shenzhen pilot zone obtain them under the 2024 MIIT pilot opening value-added telecom services to foreign investment? Is a provincial or national licence needed, and what are the capital requirements?
3. **Messaging and profiles.** Do contract-scoped messaging between client and engineer, or visible pseudonymized engineer profiles, fall into B25 sub-categories not opened by the pilot (e.g. instant information interaction, information community platforms, information search)? Do they trigger algorithm filing or any other CAC filing?
4. **Framework contract.** How should the framework agreement between the Hong Kong company and the Partner be structured (our draft will be provided) so that the Partner is a genuine buyer and seller and not a payment intermediary? We also need to know how it should register technology-export contracts (framework agreement or each statement of work), apply VAT zero-rating under Article 9 of the VAT Law Implementing Regulations, withhold IIT (including whether the cumulative method of SAT Announcement 2025 No. 16 is available to it) and obtain valid pre-tax deduction vouchers for payments to individuals.
5. **Decree 810 and PIPL Article 53.** Is a Hong Kong-operated platform serving mainland engineers an overseas internet platform enterprise subject to the reporting duties of State Council Decree No. 810? Is the platform within PIPL Article 3(2), so that Article 53 requires a designated representative? Can the Partner act as both the reporting agent and the representative, and what liability would it assume?
6. **Cross-border data.** Which transfer mechanism applies to engineer profiles and CVs that the Hong Kong company collects directly and shows to overseas clients? The options are the exemptions under the 2024 Provisions on Promoting and Regulating Cross-Border Data Flows, the standard contract, certification (effective 1 January 2026) or a security assessment. Please consider this in light of the CAC's July 2026 Q&A on résumés in recruitment. Does direct collection count towards the annual thresholds, and is our consent design adequate?
7. **Prohibited categories and reviews.** Which further project categories should be prohibited (e.g. under the export-control catalogue, the important-data rules or other national-security rules)? How should project reviews and engineer declarations be documented to protect the engineers and the Partner?
8. **Individual business registration.** Should frequent engineers register as individual industrial and commercial households (个体工商户) and invoice the Partner? How can this be done without increasing labour-relationship or tax-recharacterization risk?
9. **Pilot zone.** Which pilot zone best suits the phase-2 WFOE (Shenzhen options first, with Shanghai Lingang for comparison)? What incentives are available, and what timeline and capital are realistic?
10. **Full-time engagements.** For a client without a China entity that wants an engineer full-time, what is the safest model (EOR through a licensed HR service company, labour dispatch, employment by our WFOE, or another option)? What permanent-establishment risk would the client and our Hong Kong company face?

**Deliverable.** A written memorandum in English with an executive summary in Chinese. For each question it should give a short answer, the analysis with citations, a risk rating (high / medium / low), the licences or filings required, and the changes we should make to the product or the contracts. The engagement includes a 60-minute kick-off call and a 60-minute debrief.

**Optional items (please quote separately).** (a) Mark-up of our draft framework agreement in Chinese and English. (b) Review of our engineer deliverables agreement and consent texts. (c) Draft PIPL representative appointment letter. (d) Support with the first technology-export contract registration.

**Timeline.** Proposal by [date, 7 business days from sending]. Memo within 4 weeks of receiving our information pack; debrief in week 5.

**What we will provide** after a conflict check and under NDA: a two-page business overview; a data-flow diagram and data inventory; the platform's compliance rules (job-posting ban, prohibited categories, pseudonymization, consent types, annual transfer counter); our three draft contracts (client services, engineer deliverables, framework agreement); assumed pilot volumes (about 30 engineers and 10 clients over 9 months, to be confirmed); target markets; and the planned payment flow.

**Please include in your proposal:** the fixed fee (total and per question); exclusions and assumptions; hourly rates for out-of-scope work; the offices and roles involved; relevant experience (anonymized is fine); confirmation that you have no conflict; languages; payment terms; and your standard engagement terms.

We would appreciate a reply through this channel. Thank you for considering this request.

Kind regards,
[Founder name], founder, Qiao

- 由一家（拟设立的）香港公司向客户销售服务，并就每个里程碑向客户收取预付款（通过 Stripe）。
- 第一阶段，由一家境内软件外包企业（下称"合作方"）作为每位工程师服务的真实采购方：以交付成果为基础与工程师签约（无固定工时、不排他），以人民币支付报酬并代扣代缴个人所得税，负责实名核验和技术出口合同登记，并按成本加成向香港公司开具服务出口发票。合作方不从事代收代付。
- 平台**只发布项目，不发布招聘信息**：每个项目均有里程碑、交付成果和结束日期（最长12个月）。客户只能看到去标识化的工程师资料，完整简历须工程师按项目单独同意后方可共享；证件和银行信息仅由合作方在境内保存。

- 第二阶段如试点成功，拟在深圳试点区域设立外商独资企业（WFOE）承接合作方的职能。

8. **个体工商户：** 高频承接项目的工程师是否宜登记为个体工商户并向合作方开票？如何在不增加劳动关系认定风险和税目重新定性风险的前提下实施？
