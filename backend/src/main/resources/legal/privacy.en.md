## Who processes your data
The Qiao platform is operated by our Hong Kong company and hosted on Alibaba Cloud in Hong Kong. Engineers' contracts and payments are handled by our partner company in mainland China.

## What we collect
- Account data: email address, role, language, sign-in times.
- Engineer profile: alias, headline, skills, years of experience, province-level region, language levels, indicative rate, availability, and, only if you provide them, CV text, education and portfolio links.
- Client company data: company name, country, website, size and declarations.
- Contract data: documents you accept, milestones, messages, files, payments and settlements.
- Technical data: IP address and browser user agent for consents, logins and security.

**We do not collect** ID card numbers, bank card numbers, face images or real names. The partner company verifies engineers' identity directly and we only keep its reference.

## How engineer data is shared
Clients only see a **pseudonymized profile** (alias, headline, skills, experience, region, languages, rate, availability, rating). Your email, CV, education and portfolio are shown to a client only if you grant "share full CV" for that specific project; you can withdraw it at any time. Every time a client is shown your data we record it.

## Why we use it
To run the marketplace, review accounts and projects, prepare contracts, process payments and monthly settlements, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations.

## Retention
Transactional records (contracts, payments, settlements, audit logs) are kept for three years. When you delete your account, other personal data is deleted or anonymized.

## Your rights
You can access and export your data, correct it, withdraw consents and delete your account (except while a contract is active) from your account settings.
