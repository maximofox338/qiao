Qiao's servers are located in **Hong Kong**, outside mainland China. By giving this consent you agree that the personal information you provide to Qiao is transferred to and processed in Hong Kong by our Hong Kong company.

- **Data:** your email address, profile (alias, headline, skills, experience, province-level region, language levels, rate, availability) and, only if you add them, CV, education and portfolio links; your applications, contracts, messages, files and earnings.
- **Purposes:** reviewing your profile, showing your pseudonymized profile to client companies of projects you apply to, preparing contracts, and calculating the monthly settlement that our partner company in mainland China pays you.
- **Recipients:** client companies see only your pseudonymized profile, unless you share your full CV for a specific project. The partner company receives your alias, partner reference, email and settlement amounts.
- **Your rights:** you can access, export and correct your data, withdraw this consent and delete your account in the app. Withdrawing this consent stops new profile reviews and applications, but does not affect processing already carried out or contracts in progress.
