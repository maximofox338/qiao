## 1. What Qiao is
Qiao is a curated marketplace for **projects with milestones**. It is not a job board and it does not create employment relationships. Client companies outside mainland China publish projects; independent engineers in China apply to them.

## 2. Who you contract with
- **Clients** buy the project service from our Hong Kong company (the "Ltd"), which issues invoices and collects prepayment for each milestone.
- **Engineers** contract with and are paid by our partner company in mainland China (the "Partner"), in RMB, with individual income tax withheld as required by law.
- Clients and engineers do not sign employment contracts with each other or with Qiao.

## 3. Projects, not jobs
Every project must have at least one milestone with a concrete deliverable and a due date, an end date, and a duration of at most 12 months. Salaries, working hours, office attendance, exclusivity and similar employment terms are not allowed. Wording that suggests employment is flagged for manual review, and our team may reject or ask to change any project.

## 4. Prohibited work
Projects in prohibited categories (including cryptocurrency, semiconductors and chip design, drones, mapping and geodata, defense, government or state-owned data, expert networks and data collection, critical minerals and battery technology, biometric or genomic data) cannot be published. Clients declare for every project that it involves no controlled technology, no bulk sensitive personal data of US persons, no employment and no prohibited category.

## 5. Payments and fees
Clients prepay each milestone before work starts. The price shown to clients includes the platform fee. Funds for a milestone are released to the engineer's settlement only after the client accepts the deliverable or a dispute is resolved. Engineers are paid monthly by the Partner.

## 6. Reviews and accounts
Client companies, engineer profiles and projects are reviewed before they become active. We may suspend accounts that break these terms, fail sanctions screening or provide false information.

## 7. Data and deletion
Personal data is handled as described in the Privacy Policy. You can export your data and delete your account at any time, except while you have an active contract. Transactional records are kept for three years.

## 8. Law
These terms are governed by the laws of the Hong Kong SAR.
