> **Draft pending legal review.**

On behalf of the company I represent, I declare that:

1. The company information provided is accurate, and the company is not owned or controlled by, or acting for, any person or entity subject to sanctions. I accept that Qiao screens the company against sanctions lists and may refuse or end the service.
2. **No controlled technology.** Our projects will not require the export, transfer or development of technology, software or data controlled under export-control laws (for example the US EAR or ITAR, or Chinese export-control laws).
3. **No bulk US sensitive data.** Our projects will not give engineers access to bulk sensitive personal data of US persons or to US government-related data.
4. **No employment.** We engage project deliverables, not employees: we will not set salaries, working hours, office attendance or exclusivity, and we will not treat engineers as our staff.
5. **No prohibited category.** Our projects will not fall into any category Qiao prohibits, including cryptocurrency, semiconductors, drones, mapping and geodata, defense, government or state-owned data, expert networks and data collection, critical minerals and battery technology, or biometric or genomic data.
6. We will keep all communication and payments for Qiao projects on the platform.

We will confirm declarations 2 to 5 again for every project we submit.
