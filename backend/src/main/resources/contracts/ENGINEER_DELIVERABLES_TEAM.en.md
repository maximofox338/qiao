## 2. How the work is done
The Contractor is paid for deliverables, not for time. There are **no fixed working hours, no place of work and no exclusivity**: the Contractor decides how, when and where to work and may work for others. This agreement creates no employment relationship with the Company, the end client, Qiao or the other team members, and no partnership between the team members.

## 3. Acceptance and payment
A milestone is payable once the end client accepts its deliverable or a dispute is resolved in the team's favour. Accepted milestones are included in the Company's monthly settlement and the Contractor's share is paid in RMB to the Contractor's own verified account, converted at the settlement rate, after withholding individual income tax as required by law.

## 4. Declarations
The Contractor confirms the engineer declaration made on the platform, in particular that no employer, non-compete or confidentiality obligation prevents this work and that no state secrets or export-controlled technology will be used.

## 5. Intellectual property and confidentiality
Upon payment of a milestone, all rights in the Contractor's contributions to its deliverables are assigned to the Company, which may transfer them to the end client. The Contractor keeps the project information confidential during the project and for three years after it.
