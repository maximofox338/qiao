## 3. Prepayment and acceptance
The Client prepays each milestone through the platform before work on it starts. After a deliverable is submitted the Client either accepts it, requests a revision with reasons, or opens a dispute. ## 4. Nature of the engagement
This is an agreement for project deliverables. It creates no employment relationship between the Client and any professional: the Client will not set salaries, working hours, office attendance or exclusivity, and will not engage the professional outside the platform for the same work during the project.

## 5. Client declarations
The Client confirms the declarations made for this project: no controlled technology, no bulk sensitive personal data of US persons, no employment, and no prohibited category.

## 6. Intellectual property and confidentiality
Upon full payment of a milestone, all rights in its deliverables are assigned to the Client. Each party keeps the other party's confidential information confidential during the project and for three years after it.
